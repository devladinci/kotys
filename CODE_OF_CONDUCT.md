# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Keep discussions focused on the project.

Unacceptable behaviour: harassment, personal attacks, trolling, publishing
others' private information without consent, or any conduct a reasonable
person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project maintainers.
All complaints are reviewed and may result in a warning, temporary or
permanent ban from project spaces, at the maintainers' discretion.
